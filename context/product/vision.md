# Vision

Sokong is the product. Mara is how it measures.

A country becomes great through good people. Good people grow up in family units, and they later start family units of their own. Mara measures a nation by how its family units are doing, not by a slogan or by GDP alone. Sokong is the long work of finding family units that are struggling or deteriorating, learning their story, and supporting them until they thrive.

The app is the record and the coordination layer for that work. It records help that people and organizations give, so the same help is not given twice. Community members, community workers, benefactors, facilitators, and nation planners use it. A family uses it to enrol and to see what happens next. The first operator is a community worker. The web client will be Nuxt and the mobile client will be Flutter. Both can be localized, and the first language is English. Both will share one Rails API. Neither client exists yet. The API's only behavior today is `GET /up`.

The line on the source document is "Good People, Great Country." Sokong means "to support." Mara means "to move forward."
