# Intent

Usamah confirmed this statement on 2026-11-17.

- Outcome: A community worker sets the first visit, records the basket, the consent, the identity check, and the story, then marks the family qualified or disqualified. That finishes Verification. Qualified moves the family into Empowerment.
- User: The community worker, and the person who enrolled, including someone enrolling many families.
- Why now: An enrolment can be submitted, and no visit can be set or marked yet.
- Success: The person who enrolled sees the visit time, and later sees qualified or disqualified. When the enrolment has an email, that address gets both messages. Each message names the head and the place. A changed visit time sends a new email and leaves the earlier one as it was.
- Constraint: Those four records have to be in before either mark. Within a week is the aim, not a block. A disqualified family stays on record.
- Out of scope: Needs, offers, and matching. Duplicate enrolments (F38). Changing a mark after it is set. The other members, the phone, the basket, the identity check, and the story in the email.

The fields inside the story are for the spec. The ongoing path stays F09. The four Mara boxes stay F08.
