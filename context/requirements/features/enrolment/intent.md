# Intent

Confirmed by Usamah on 2026-11-02 with "Yes. All good."

- Outcome: A family can enrol itself from a phone, or anyone can enrol them on behalf. The record tells a community worker who the family is, where they are, how to find them, and what help they are asking for. With an account, the submitter sees who has looked, what they said, and when the first visit is.
- User: The person who submits — the family, a community member, a community worker, or anyone else — and the community worker who has to contact and meet the family.
- Why now: Sokong cannot take an enrolment yet. A visit, an assessment, and any help all wait on this record.
- Success: The enrolment is in with the enroler's contact, a location, and at least one named member, even when the family has no phone and no locality or worker exists yet. Before anyone has looked, the submitter with an account sees that the case is in and that no visit is set.
- Constraint: Creating an account must not hold the enrolment off. An optional email only invites them to come back, sign up, and then view progress. A toggle marks self-enrolment or on-behalf enrolment. In both, consent is confirmed at the verification visit. Self-enrolment says submission implies the family's consent, confirmed at that visit. On behalf, the case is valid and will be checked at a visit, and it may still not be enrolled unless the family consents then.
- Form: Longer answers stay visible and can be empty. The form asks for the head and members (name, age, gender, a short description, in education, working, or both, and if working the job and the rough monthly amount that comes into the family), an optional family phone, free text on how to locate and reach them, and the kind of help. Help starts from defaults such as food, clothing, school supplies, and help applying to a body such as social security, welfare, or a tithe body, and they can name a help that is not in that list.
- Locality: A location is always specified. Device location can suggest the nearest district. The screen says that choice came from the current location, and they can name a different place because they may not be with the family. If that place has no locality, the enrolment creates one. If it fits more than one, it goes to a locality that has community workers, and if several do, the closest. If localities fit and none have a community worker, the closest is used.
- Out of scope: The verification visit itself, the four checkboxes, qualifying the case, giving the help, mail on every progress change, and accounts for benefactors, facilitators, and planners.

Specs and plans consume this file. They do not replace it.
